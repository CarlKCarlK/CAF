About Civil Liberty Index

CLI is an index to "newsy" netnews articles about civil liberties. It is available via a mailing list or via the world wide web.

Articles are retrieved from your local news server. This avoids copyright problems with the UPI articles from Clarinet. It also means that you will not be able to read articles from newsgroups your sites does not carry.

To help when articles have expired or haven't appeared on your system yet, related articles are also listed.
