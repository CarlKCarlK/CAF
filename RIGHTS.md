# Rights and Reuse Note

This repository preserves historical Computers and Academic Freedom (CAF) archive materials originally hosted by EFF. The archive contains materials from many authors and institutions, including reposts, pointers, quotations, policies, public statements, and documents included for research and educational purposes.

This repository does not assert new copyright ownership over those historical materials. Rights remain with their original authors or publishers where applicable.

The preservation copy is intended to document the historical CAF archive and make it easier to study. If you are a rights holder and have concerns about a specific file, please open an issue or contact the repository maintainer.
