CICA Removed Fanny Hill (was Re: Censorship of Fanny Hill...)
  1. jan_09_1994#CJ8EBF.87E@cs.uiuc.edu 
    CICA Removed Fanny Hill (was Re: Censorship of Fanny Hill...)
    [paraphrase: Here is an overview of the legal argument against the
    exclusion/removal: The First Amendment applies to the states, to state
    universities, and to state university employees when they are acting
    within the scope of their employement. In the _Island Tree v. Pico_
    (1982) Supreme Court case '"four of the nine justices said that
    school boards may not remove books from the school library shelves
    simply because they dislike the (sexual) ideas contained in those
    books." (4 said no. 1 didn't say.)']
  2. jan_09_1994#CJ7BrI.FwF@cs.uiuc.edu 
    CICA Removed Fanny Hill (was Re: Censorship of Fanny Hill...)
    [paraphrase: Contrary to stated policy, the CICA archive contains
    other "adult" material. I found a passage about a "whore" whose lovers
    were hung like donkeys and who ejaculated like horses. Other "adult"
    passages are enclosed. The source? the CICA archives's on-line
    versions of the Bible.]
  3. jan_09_1994#CJAE31.8Iu@usenet.ucs.indiana.edu 
    CICA Removed Fanny Hill (was Re: Censorship of Fanny Hill...)
    [paraphrase: A previous article said a university should not select
    material for a general archive on the basis of what is appropriate for
    grade school children. I agree. But Indiana U. 'does not put "adult"
    material into an archive that can be accessed by minors. Period.'
    (According the circulation desk at the Indiana U. library, you must be
    16 years or older and have a driver's license or other ID to use the
    collection.) American Library Association recommendations can "not
    negate local laws".]
  4. jan_09_1994#2gj1e8$r50@eff.org 
    CICA Removed Fanny Hill (was Re: Censorship of Fanny Hill...)
    [paraphrase: Not all reason for excluding or removing material are
    equivalent. "Deleting a file for lack of space does not have the same
    censorship significance as deleting a file because its content may be
    offensive to some."]
  5. jan_09_1994#1994Jan8.174254.13856@hellgate.utah.edu 
    CICA Removed Fanny Hill (was Re: Censorship of Fanny Hill...)
    [paraphrase: A reply to the claim that 'IU does not provide
    "adult" materials to minors'. A 16-year old is a minor so the
    claim is false. Also, even though "a 10 year old is not allowed to
    check out the book, [...]  they can still walk in," and read or
    photocopy a book.]

  2. cafv01n44#1991Dec16.154149.15030@eff.org 
    Notes 8-9 try to answer frequently asked questions.

8. Enclosed are answers (with references) to the questions "Should my
university remove Netnews newsgroups because some people find them
offensive?"  and "If it doesn't have the resources to carry all
newsgroups, how should newsgroups be selected?"
  3. cafv01n44#1991Dec16.191620.21567@eff.org 
    Notes 2-5 are about Iowa State University's Netnews policy.

4. "The Iowa State University policy should, in my opinion, be changed
to better respect intellectual freedom by more accurately reflecting
library policy (and the law)." It violates the principles of at least
three American Library Association policy statements. (These are
enclosed.)
