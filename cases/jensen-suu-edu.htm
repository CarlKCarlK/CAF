[This is a note from the Southern Utah University student who was expelled from the computer lab. - Carl]

Confessions of a Civilly Disobedient Student

I confess that I "tricked' SUU into enforcing their vague
and probably illegal computer policy.

I went to the computer lab and read material that I thought
might violate the policy. I then asked other students in the lab
to denounce what I was reading.

I did not know what punishment I might face. Under the policy
almost any punishment was possible.

The punishment I received was summary expulsion from the lab.
The lab assistant who expelled me now says that his was motivated
only in part by my choice of reading material. He says he also expelled
me because he thought, without me saying so to him, that I wanted to
be expelled.

I did not want to be expelled just for the sake of expulsion.
What I wanted was to test the policy and demonstrate its
unfairness. A speech code that is never enforced can still censor
by chilling speech. I hoped that the heat from my civilly disobedient
act would melt that chill.

Michaun Jensen
