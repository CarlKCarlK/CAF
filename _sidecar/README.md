# Sidecar Materials

This directory contains contextual material related to the history of the Computers and Academic Freedom Project.

These files were not part of the original CAF archive. They come from Carl Kadie's related email, Usenet, generated research indexes, and personal research archives, and are included here to support historical reconstruction, citation, and commentary.

Current sections:

- `selected_email/` - selected source email threads useful for citation, including the March 1991 CAF proposal to Mitch Kapor and John Perry Barlow, the Feb. 1996 Pacifica/CDA Fight Censorship post, the Aug. 1996 Princeton ACLU letter item, and an excerpt of Computer underground Digest #6.31 (Apr. 1994) on the FBI's mistaken detentions of two attendees at CFP '94.
- `selected_usenet/` - individual Usenet posts from 1991-1997 cited in the CAF history article, recovered via usenet-rewind.com (a Usenet archive search service) and stored here so they can be linked from a stable location. Includes the July 1991 CAF "Help Wanted" post, a 1991 email-ethics apology, Bob Solon's "I've changed my mind" post, the 1992 "History of alt groups" reply, the 1992 open letter to the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, the 1992 "Best academic policy: privacy" nomination, a 1992 reply about the banned-material list, John Hascall's 1992 post on Iowa State's newsgroup policy, and four press reposts: Chris Trimble's Nov 1991 post quoting a _Daily Illini_ article on the Steven Brack case, _eye WEEKLY_ (Toronto) on the Homolka ban (May 1994), _The New York Times_ "The Next Front in the Book Wars" (Nov 1994), and the _San Francisco Chronicle_ on SUNY Binghamton (May 1997). Also: Carl's Nov 1994 repost, with permission, of Martin Rimm's email about the CMU ban ("The CMU administration has not read the report!") and Declan McCullagh's Jan 1996 post citing that repost in the Rimm/Sirbu debate; Carl's Jan 1995 repost of Julian Assange's Deakin University complaint; and Adam Back's Aug 1995 post on the Perl/RSA "munitions" T-shirts, as distributed in the CAF-Talk digest. Also five posts from the Feb-Aug 1993 SRI anonymous-FTP dispute: the Memphis State student's original report, Carl's 7 Feb 1993 post, Carl's 15 Feb 1993 "form letter contest," Doug Moran's 17 Feb 1993 reply announcing that SRI had turned off anonymous FTP, and Moran's Aug 1993 critique of Carl's handling. Later items: Carl's Feb 1998 repost of the AAUP report "Academic Freedom and Electronic Communications," and two Dec 2000 CAF-Talk posts from after AutoCAD users adopted the group (Seth Finkelstein explaining that "acad" meant "academic," and Wayne Rhine's "salvaged a derelict" reply).
- `usenet/` - recovered Usenet/news source material and generated sidecar indexes.
- `research/` - generated research metadata and indexes used to reconstruct the archive and its history.
